# What is STARPGS?
>Officially formed in August 2025, STARPGS is a small, independent game studio striving to combine the best elements of both modern and retro RPGs to create the best possible experiences. In future posts, you can follow the development of Astral Paradise, the first project in the works. Stay tuned for more!
