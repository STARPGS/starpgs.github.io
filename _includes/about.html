Astral Paradise

"The true threat is not this pursuit's impossibility, but whether one emerges from this path's darkness as themselves."


The first project by STARPGS! Currently in development. Astral Paradise is an RPG combining the best elements of both modern and retro RPGs, with a high emphasis on strategic battles, build customization, meaningful exploration, thought-provoking storytelling, realistic character interactions, interesting side-quests, and an OST that captures the feeling of the old greats. Split-screen couch co-op is planned!
