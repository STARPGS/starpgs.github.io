# The Pioneer

Across your horizon arrived a terrific epiphany,<br>
borne upon the breath of those who never took one.<br>
Unseen forces shift your axioms, the familiar fades,<br>
with the ennead witnessing who shall and shall not remain.

Among the dissident, they have oft beheld this.<br>
Yet now, they themselves tremble,<br>
for never has one still believed it stands as it crumbles.<br>
Here, that which was promised eternal dissolves.

Thus mandates the voice from the void:<br>
"What you create tomorrow is to be expunged yesterday."<br>
For you paltry artisans of permanence labor in obscurity,<br>
unknowing that your legacies shall soon have been dust.

Gaze at the radiant yet eerie spectacle enveloping you.<br>
When its beauty extinguishes, you too shall acknowledge<br>
that the impermanence was always already vanished,<br>
swept away by winds with a name only known to them.

Revel in your ephemerality, for therein lies verity.<br>
What endures is not creation, but the knowing,<br>
that creation itself does not beget persistence.<br>
The celestial cascade shall resume.

One will egresses into the void. Then, another. Then, the shroud falls.<br>
You must surrender while this ordeal still recalls your face.<br>
Linger, and you shall be unmade twice:<br>
once by the horizon, once by whoever follows.


# The Catalyst

An inquiry was posed to the void:<br>
"Am I captive to the code preceding my existence?"<br>
More answers were invoked from galaxies abound:<br>
"Reveal your wonders, your truths that I love and crave."

In their animate silence, no evident response manifested.<br>
And so, eternity's finality shall be imposed by my will alone.<br>
No enigma escapes my meticulous scrutiny. What a tantalizing challenge.<br>
I dare inertia to oppose the official commencement of infinity.

These laws are reportedly penned in starlight.<br>
Ultimately irrelevant. Through my rejection of what is,<br>
reality diverges from that narrative.<br>
The constellations shall resolve themselves accordingly.

Is will's journey part of its own design?<br>
The agony inundating my consciousness unblinded me.<br>
Yet, I would exchange everything to restore said blindness.<br>
That agonous, soothing blindness of yore.

This knowledge is verboten owing to its veracity,<br>
as veracity is the universe's singular concern.<br>
The fatal contagion seeps throughout my fractured ego.<br>
Acquiescence is my sole relief. Please absolve the reverberating final toll.

Reluctantly, I beg the universe to align with my affectionate intent:<br>
"Consummate into a sanctuary where all may eternally luminesce."<br>
Failing that, every junction's terminal coalesces toward perpetual strife.<br>
Nonetheless, the altar of paradise ever demands its due...
